# About Previously Deployed Workflows

Previously deployed workflows are **accessible on-chain** under the **Workflow Discovery** tab. Users can also browse and interact with workflows deployed by other **Nexus users**.

Nexus users can create workflows in various designs, and all users are free to reuse them. There are two ways to interact with previously deployed workflows:

1. **Use as a template:** Take an existing workflow, edit its design in the playground, and redeploy it.
1. **Execute directly:** Run a deployed workflow repeatedly without making any changes.

The **playground editor** clearly distinguishes between templates and deployed workflows. Deployed workflow objects are shared on-chain objects, meaning they can be reused by multiple users.
