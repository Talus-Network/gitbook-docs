## Welcome to Talus Vision&#x20;

### 1. Blank Workflow

If you're already familiar with Talus Vision, you can start directly with an empty editor and build from scratch.

### 2. Quickstart Template

Loads a [sample template](https://docs.talus.network/getting-started/math-branching-quickstart) so you can quickly explore Talus Vision. This includes documentation that helps you understand the basics of Nexus and its workflow structure.

### 3. Browse Templates

Redirects you to workflows previously created by other Nexus users, allowing you to review and reuse any of them as templates for your own projects.

## What Happens Next

Once you choose an option, Talus Vision opens the workflow canvas, where you can start building or exploring. You can also access your previously deployed or saved workflows at any time from the sidebar.
